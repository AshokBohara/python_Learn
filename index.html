📚 Admission FAQs

What are the eligibility criteria?
Minimum 45% in +2/equivalent with Mathematics. Entrance exam must be cleared.
When does admission start?
Admissions open twice yearly: March-April and August-September.

💵 Fee Structure

What is the total fee?
Computer: NPR 600,000 | Civil: NPR 550,000 | Electrical: NPR 570,000
Are scholarships available?
Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships available for top performers.

🏛️ Campus Facilities

What labs are available?
Computer, Electronics, Civil, Mechanical labs with modern equipment.
Hostel facilities?
Separate hostels for boys & girls with WiFi, cafeteria, and security.
